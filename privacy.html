Browsing and contacting us

You can explore our books without creating an account. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and keep relevant correspondence.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is available on this website, and loads only if you accept analytics cookies.

When enabled, Google Analytics 4 helps us understand visits, pages viewed, and general device and browser information. We load Google Analytics only after you accept analytics cookies. Refusing does not affect your access to the website.

We do not enable Google advertising signals or advertising personalization in this integration. We do not send the contents of your emails to Google Analytics. Google may process analytics information on servers outside your country. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your cookie choices

Once analytics is enabled, you can accept or reject analytics cookies. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice at any time. We remember your choice in this browser for up to six months using local storage. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics collection from this site and removes its accessible Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase information already collected.

The analytics cookies used by this integration have a maximum lifetime of one year. Browser settings may shorten that period. Your cookie choice is stored separately from those analytics cookies.
