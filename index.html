THE TILOUA STORY

Made for little minds.
And the moments you share.

A funny sound. A tiny finger pointing at a picture. The same favorite page, again and again. To a little one, these are moments of discovery. To a parent, they can become something just as meaningful.

Tiloua is a new children’s publishing imprint with a big ambition: to bring learning, play, and parent-child connection together in every book we create.

Learning begins with connection.

We believe early learning should feel like an invitation to explore. A character sparks curiosity. A playful question starts a conversation. A picture becomes a sound, a word, or a little story you make up together.

That is why educational purpose and shared interaction shape our books from the very beginning. We create opportunities for grown-ups to join in, follow their child’s curiosity, and make each reading their own. There is room for laughter, repetition, imagination, and quiet closeness.

Our mission is to support children as they discover the world and begin to find their place in it, nurturing the curiosity, confidence, and self-expression they will carry as they grow.

Little books. A growing world.

Our story begins with Discover Animals!, a collection made for sharing from birth. And this is just the beginning. We are building Tiloua for babies and toddlers, with more books planned to explore the discoveries, feelings, and everyday experiences of their early years.

Our vision is simple: thoughtfully made books that bring fresh ideas to early learning and make screen-free time together something to look forward to.

Because a book you choose for your child
can be a gift for you, too.

A moment to connect. A discovery to share. A reason to read it again.

THE ART BEHIND THE ANIMALS

Meet Nina Marceau.

Little worlds for curious little minds.

Nina Marceau is the illustrator behind Tiloua’s Discover Animals! collection. Her illustrations bring together simple shapes, expressive animals, and playful details, inviting little ones and their grown-ups to explore each page together.

From a curious tiger to a smiling sea turtle, each animal brings its own personality to the page.
